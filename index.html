An open-source audio receiver for macOS that works with AirPlay.

Pick it as the audio output on an iPhone, iPad or another computer and the sound plays on your Mac — or goes straight into your DAW. Lossless, stereo, several sources at once.

v0.2.0 macOS 13+ Apple Silicon & Intel Free & open source · AGPL
Download for macOS universal · ~10 MB
View source on GitHub →

WHAT IT DOES

⇆

Several sources at once

Every sender gets its own slot with fader, level meters, mute and solo — mixed into one output.

♪

Now playing + remote

Track, artist, album and artwork from each sender, with play/pause, next and previous sent back.

●

Sampling

Record the clean incoming mix and drag the WAV straight into Ableton, Finder or any other app.

▸

Straight into your DAW

Choose any output device, including loopback drivers like BlackHole, and record AirPlay audio on a track.

≋

Buffer modes

Sync, Short or Monitor latency profiles, from rock-solid playback to tighter monitoring.

⚿

Access control

Open to everyone, protected by a password, or approving each new device by hand — your call.

INSTALL IN A MINUTE

1

Download the zip above, unzip it and move Tape In.app to Applications.

2

This build is not notarized yet, so macOS warns on first launch. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, or clear the quarantine flag:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Tape In.app"
3

Allow the Local Network permission when asked, then pick Tape In in the AirPlay menu of your iPhone. That's it.
